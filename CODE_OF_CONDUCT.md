# Code of conduct

PyTomography follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and help newcomers. Harassment, personal attacks and
discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, Discourse or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at lukepolson@outlook.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
